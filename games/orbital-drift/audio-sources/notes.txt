PROCESSING AND VALIDATION
prepare-audio.py reproduces all selected files using FFmpeg. Original audio,
and original archive licenses remain unmodified under originals/. Source evidence
is retained under evidence/; transient anonymous theme/form tokens in the
OpenGameArt snapshot were redacted before publication (see manifest.json). manifest.json records sources, download date, transformations,
format metadata and SHA-256 hashes for original, evidence and selected files.

The original music contains about 1.3 seconds of trailing silence; the prepared
loop removes this and uses a 0.6-second linear overlap at the seam. Its gain is
reduced to 0.55 for headroom. The thruster loop uses a 0.05-second overlap.
All prepared files decode successfully with FFmpeg and retain 44.1 kHz sampling.

No actual listening was performed by the asset-research agent. Selection is
based on the authors' descriptions and asset names, waveform/silence metrics,
and technical decoding checks. The music's subjective fit and absence of vocals
have not been verified by listening. A seam crossfade is an editing step, not
a claim that the musical phrasing has been auditioned as seamless.
preview.html is provided for human listening before final sound-mix sign-off.

INTEGRATION STARTING POINTS
Music should stay quiet behind the engine and effects. Consider music gain
0.25–0.4, UI gain 0.25, explosion gain 0.45, and thruster gain 0.1–0.2;
these are initial values to adjust during actual play/listening, not a tested mix.
Start sound only after a user gesture, provide separate music and SFX toggles,
and stop the thruster during pause, game over, focus loss and hidden-tab states.
